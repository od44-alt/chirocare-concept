How we can help

Several chiropractors, massage therapists and an athletic therapist in one clinic, so the right person sees you and harder cases get more than one set of eyes.

All services
  • Chiropractic careSpinal adjustments, soft tissue therapy, and rehabilitative exercise tailored to each patient — for back and neck pain, headaches, sciatica, posture, and more.
  • Massage therapyWorks alongside chiropractic to address muscle patterns, improve circulation and tone, and support recovery. Swedish, remedial, and deep tissue available.
  • Athletic therapyAssessment and rehab for sprains, strains, overuse injuries, and return-to-sport plans — for recreational and competitive athletes.
  • OsteopathyManual osteopathic care including craniosacral and visceral approaches, offered by Becky Adams, DOMP.
  • AcupunctureAvailable with Dr. Shapiro (Acupuncture Canada / ACC). Ask when booking which practitioners offer acupuncture.
  • Car accident / MPI carePrompt assessment after motor vehicle accidents. MPI may cover initial exams and treatment when you seek care in a reasonable time.

Been in a car accident?

Book an assessment promptly. MPI may cover initial exams and treatment when you seek care within a reasonable time; waiting can put coverage at risk.

Meet some of the team

Full team

  • Dr. Sean Shapiro

    Chiropractor · Founder

    BSc, DC, FICPA, CSCS, ACC

    Founded CHIROCARE in 2008 on Taylor Avenue. Fellowship with the International Chiropractic Pediatric Association, Webster Technique, CSCS, and licensed acupuncturist with Acupuncture Canada. Introduced to chiropractic after a car accident as a teen — both he and his brother became chiropractors.

    RMT
