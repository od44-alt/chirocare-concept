Your first visit

When you come to CHIROCARE you’ll be greeted by one of our assistants and made to feel at home. New patients often remark that our office feels unlike other doctors’ offices — by design. Our goal is state-of-the-art chiropractic care in a comfortable surrounding.

Make yourself at home

Arrive with ease
Convenient designated parking right in front of our door. You’ll be greeted by name, given a short tour, shown a short (~4-minute) video, and once intake paperwork is done you’ll meet the doctor.

We explain everything first
The consultation discusses your health goals and whether chiropractic is likely to help. If it looks promising, a thorough examination helps identify the cause of your problem. Patients appreciate that we explain everything in advance — no surprises.
What new patients typically experience
A detailed consultation, spinal assessment, and discussion of health goals. The chiropractor reviews posture, range of motion, and any prior imaging. Most patients receive their first adjustment during this visit.
Typical appointment length
About 15–30 minutes depending on the condition and plan. Initial consultations take longer to include assessment and discussion.
