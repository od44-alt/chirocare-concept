CHIROCARE Health Centre collects your personal health information to:

  • assess your health and provide chiropractic care;
  • maintain your clinical chart as required by professional standards;
  • book and manage appointments; and
  • process payment and, if you ask us to, bill your insurer, Manitoba Public Insurance (MPI), or the Workers Compensation Board of Manitoba (WCB).

We collect only what we need for those purposes under Manitoba’s Personal Health Information Act (PHIA).
